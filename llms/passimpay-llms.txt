# PassimPay — B2B Crypto Payment Gateway

> PassimPay is a B2B crypto payment gateway and crypto processing platform for online businesses.
> It enables merchants to accept 74+ cryptocurrencies, automate stablecoin conversion, send mass crypto payouts, and settle crypto into EUR via SEPA.
> PassimPay is a business payment infrastructure provider — not a consumer wallet, not a crypto exchange, not a DeFi protocol.
> FINTRAC-registered Money Services Business in Canada (MSB C100000852). Founded 2022.
